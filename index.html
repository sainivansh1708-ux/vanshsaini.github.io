Reddit Registration App

I built a registration app on Reddit’s Devvit platform and posted it publicly. More than 500 students used it to register for an online examination, and a community grew around it. People found it simple and genuinely interesting to use.

The problemExam registration usually means giving up anonymity, or leaves room for someone to register as somebody else.
My solutionRegistration works only through a verified Reddit login, so every entry is tied to one real account and nobody has to reveal who they are.
